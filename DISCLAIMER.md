## 2. No Hosting or Storage of Copyrighted Content
- **Zero Media Storage:** SongPlay **does not host, store, cache, upload, or re-broadcast** any audio files, MP3s, M4As, videos, or media content on its servers or repository.
- **Dynamic Third-Party Indexing:** All audio streaming links, metadata, and album artwork displayed or accessed through SongPlay are retrieved dynamically in real time from third-party public Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and public search APIs.
- **Server Role:** The web server functions merely as an interface and client-side aggregator. No proprietary sound recordings exist on this infrastructure.
